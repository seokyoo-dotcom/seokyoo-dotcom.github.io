# Privacy Policy

[한국어](privacy-ko.html)

**App**: Hoink Hoink! Slide Puzzles (맷꿀맷꿀! 슬라이드 퍼즐)
**Operator**: Hoink Studios (sole proprietorship registered in Korea, business registration no. 778-40-01490, representative Seok Yoo, "we")
**Effective date**: 2026-11-15

## 1. Information we collect

We do not offer accounts and we do not directly collect your name, phone number or address.

| Category | Data | How it is collected |
|---|---|---|
| Game progress | Stage records, currencies, collected boars, settings | Stored only on your device (not sent to our servers) |
| Advertising | Advertising ID (GAID/IDFA), device model, OS version, language, approximate location (from IP), ad views and clicks | Collected automatically by ad SDKs |
| Analytics and crash reports | App instance ID, in-game events (e.g. stage cleared, purchase completed), session length, device model, OS version, crash logs | Collected automatically by Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics |
| Purchases | Purchased item, time of purchase, receipt data | Processed by the store (Google Play, App Store). We never receive your card details |
| Support | Email address, message content | Provided by you when you contact us |

## 2. How we use it

- To run the game and deliver purchased items
- To show ads and measure ad performance
- To understand how the game is played and to find and fix crashes (analytics and crash reports)
- To answer support requests and fix errors

## 3. Third parties

The following partners' SDKs process data to serve ads, measure usage and report crashes. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies.

| Partner | Privacy policy |
|---|---|
| Google AdMob (Google LLC) | <https://policies.google.com/privacy> |
| Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google LLC) | <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy> |
| Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) | <https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy> |
| Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) | <https://liftoff.io/privacy-policy/> |

If we add other ad partners through AdMob mediation, we will list them here before they go live.

Payments are processed by Google LLC and Apple Inc.

## 4. Retention

- Game progress: deleted from your device when you uninstall the app.
- Analytics data: kept by Firebase for the retention period we set (2 to 14 months); crash reports for up to 90 days.
- Support messages: 3 years after the request is closed.
- Purchase records: as long as the law requires (5 years under Korean e-commerce law).

## 5. Your choices and rights

- Personalised ads: reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your device settings. You can also change your consent in the app's privacy (ad consent) settings.
- Access and deletion: email hoinkstudios@gmail.com and we will respond within 10 days.
- Game data: uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on your device.

## 6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

## 7. International transfers

Our ad, analytics and crash-reporting partners' servers are located outside Korea (mainly in the United States), so the advertising, analytics and crash data in section 1 may be transferred abroad over the network while you use the app. The recipient, country and retention period follow each partner's privacy policy. To avoid this, decline ad consent in the app or turn off personalised ads on your device.
