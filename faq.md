# FAQ

[한국어](faq-ko.html) · [Home](./)

## Progress

**Q. What happens to my progress if I uninstall or change phones?**
Progress (stages, currencies, collected boars) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling or moving to another device starts a new game. There is no account link or cloud save at the moment.

**Q. I'm out of stamina.**
Stamina refills over time. You can also claim a free stamina gift once a day in the shop and get more by watching ads.

**Q. I'm stuck on a puzzle.**
A hint shows the next moves, and Undo takes back one move at a time.

## Chests and odds

**Q. Where can I see the chest odds?**
Tap "Odds" on the chest screen in the game, or see the [chest odds page](odds.html#chest-odds-english). Chests are free from clearing stages and can't be bought with gems or money.

## Purchases

**Q. I paid but didn't get my item.**
Fully close and reopen the game. If it still hasn't arrived, email hoinkstudios@gmail.com with your order number (Google Play order IDs start with GPA, or your Apple receipt).

**Q. How do I get a refund?**
Payments are handled by the store, so refunds are requested there.
- Google Play: [Google Play refund help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637)
- App Store: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)

**Q. Can I restore purchases?**
Every product is consumable (used up when spent), so purchases don't carry over to another device.

## Ads and privacy

**Q. Do ads pop up?**
No. The only ads are rewarded ads that you choose to watch.

**Q. How do I change my ad consent?**
Use the privacy (ad consent) option in the game settings. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your device settings.

**Q. How do I delete my data?**
Game progress is deleted when you uninstall the app. For data held by our ad and analytics partners, or any other request, email hoinkstudios@gmail.com and we will respond within 10 days. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

## Other

**Q. Which languages are supported?**
Korean, English, Russian, Portuguese (Brazil) and Vietnamese. Change it in the game settings.

**Q. I found a bug.**
Email hoinkstudios@gmail.com with your device model, the stage number and what happened. A screenshot helps.
